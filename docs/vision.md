# Vision

## What is TemanDonor?
TemanDonor is an open-source blood donation platform that connects donors, 
patients, hospitals, and blood banks through a transparent, fast, and 
community-driven system.

## Problem
- Finding blood donors in urgent situations is slow and mostly done manually via WhatsApp groups
- No real-time visibility of blood stock availability
- Donors have no way to track the impact of their donation
- Rhesus-negative blood types are especially hard to find due to rarity

## Our Solution
A centralized platform where:
- Donors register once and get notified when urgently needed
- Patients or families can request blood and track status in real-time
- Hospitals and PMI can manage requests and publish stock transparently
- The community stays connected through forums and updates

## Target Users
| User | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Pendonor | Registered blood donors ready to help |
| Pasien / Keluarga | Patients or families in need of blood |
| Admin PMI / RS | Hospital and blood bank administrators |
| Guest | Visitors browsing public information |

## Core Values
- **Transparent** — blood stock and donation status visible to all
- **Fast** — urgent notifications reach the right donor immediately  
- **Human** — built by the community, for the community
- **Open** — open-source, anyone can contribute and improve

## Long-term Vision
To become Indonesia's most trusted blood donation platform — 
starting from the Rhesus-negative community, scaling to nationwide, 
and eventually open to integration with PMI and hospitals across Indonesia.
